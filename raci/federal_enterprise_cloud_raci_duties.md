# Enterprise Cloud & On-Premises RACI Matrix Job Duties - Federal Agency

## Executive Leadership

- Strategic decision-making for cloud and on-premises infrastructure
- Approval of major cloud initiatives and investments
- Oversight of compliance with federal regulations (FISMA, FedRAMP, etc.)
- Final approval of enterprise architecture decisions
- Authorization of cloud service provider partnerships
- Sign-off on security policies and risk management frameworks
- Budget allocation for cloud and infrastructure projects
- Alignment of IT strategy with agency mission

## Cloud Program Management Office (PMO)

- Coordination of cloud adoption initiatives across departments
- Development and maintenance of cloud governance frameworks
- Tracking and reporting of cloud migration progress
- Management of cloud service provider relationships
- Cost monitoring and optimization of cloud resources
- Implementation of cloud standards and best practices
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning
- Change management and organizational readiness

## Enterprise Architects

- Design of cloud and hybrid architecture solutions
- Development of technical standards and patterns
- Evaluation of cloud service providers and technologies
- Integration planning between cloud and on-premises systems
- Creation of reference architectures
- Technology stack standardization
- Oversight of solution designs
- Guidance on architectural compliance

## Security & Compliance Team

- Implementation of security controls and policies
- Continuous monitoring of security posture
- FedRAMP compliance management
- Security assessment and authorization (A&A)
- Incident response planning and execution
- Access control management
- Security training and awareness
- Audit preparation and response

## Operations Team

- Day-to-day management of cloud and on-premises infrastructure
- System monitoring and maintenance
- Incident and problem management
- Performance optimization
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Capacity planning and management
- Service level agreement (SLA) monitoring
- Technical support and troubleshooting

## Development Team

- Cloud-native application development
- Application modernization and migration
- Integration of cloud services
- Implementation of CI/CD pipelines
- Code quality and security compliance
- Testing and validation
- Documentation maintenance
- Performance optimization

## Data Management Team

- Data migration planning and execution
- Database administration and optimization
- Data security and privacy compliance
- Data backup and recovery
- Data architecture design
- Master data management
- Data quality assurance
- Data governance implementation

## Network Team

- Network architecture design and implementation
- Connectivity between cloud and on-premises
- Network security implementation
- Performance monitoring and optimization
- DNS and load balancer management
- VPN and direct connect configuration
- Network troubleshooting
- Bandwidth management

## Finance Team

- Cloud cost management and optimization
- Budget tracking and reporting
- Financial forecasting
- Procurement management
- Vendor contract management
- ROI analysis
- Chargeback/showback implementation
- Financial compliance monitoring

## Procurement/Acquisition Team

- Cloud service provider contract management
- Vendor evaluation and selection
- Contract negotiation and renewal
- Compliance with federal acquisition regulations
- License management
- Service level agreement review
- Cost analysis and optimization
- Procurement process management

## Training & Change Management Team

- Training needs assessment
- Training program development
- User adoption strategies
- Change impact analysis
- Communications planning
- Stakeholder engagement
- Documentation and knowledge base maintenance
- Performance support development

## Quality Assurance Team

- Testing strategy development
- Quality standards implementation
- Performance testing
- Security testing
- Compliance verification
- Test automation
- Defect management
- Quality metrics tracking

## Project Management Team

- Project planning and execution
- Resource allocation
- Timeline management
- Risk management
- Stakeholder communication
- Progress tracking and reporting
- Issue resolution
- Dependencies management

## Audit & Compliance Team

- Internal audit execution
- Compliance monitoring
- Policy enforcement
- Documentation review
- Control testing
- Audit response coordination
- Regulatory requirement tracking
- Compliance reporting
